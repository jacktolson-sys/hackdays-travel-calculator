📊 ROI Decision Engine — Cost Per Lead Analysis
—
Cost Per Lead ℹ Cost Per Lead (CPL) = Total Event Budget ÷ Expected Leads Captured.

It measures how much Geotab spends to acquire one qualified contact at an event. Lower is better — a $200 CPL means every new lead cost the company $200 in event spend. Geotab uses CPL to compare event efficiency across trade shows, conferences, and hosted events.
